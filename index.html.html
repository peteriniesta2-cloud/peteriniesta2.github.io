The strike ladder

A clear, written sequence staff can read in advance, so nobody is surprised by a sanction.

  1. Strike 1
    Recorded, with a conversationThe reason is written down, the employee is spoken to, and the expected change is agreed.
  2. Strike 2
    Final warningA letter is issued. The employee is told plainly that one more strike leads to review.
  3. Strike 3
    Termination reviewThe case goes to a review with the full record in front of the decision maker.

Strikes older than the reset window (3 months in our default setup) stop counting, so staff who improve are not punished forever.

    Common questions

    Do I need to be a big company to use HR consulting?

    No. Most of our clients are small teams who want clear rules and a fair way of managing people before problems grow.

    What does the first conversation cost?

    Nothing. We use it to understand your situation and tell you honestly whether and how we can help.

    Can you work with my team remotely?

    Yes. Most of the work can be done by WhatsApp, calls and shared documents, with in-person sessions where useful.

    Will you help us write policies we can actually enforce?

    Yes. We write them with your managers, in simple language, and agree how each rule will be applied before it goes live.
